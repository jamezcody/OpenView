# Sources and acquisition behavior

## Park overlays (local)

The local park overlays use the [official NPS administrative boundary dataset](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/administrative-boundaries-of-national-park-system-units-national-geospatial-data-asset-ngd) and [USGS PAD-US 4.1](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/gap-analysis-project/science/pad-us-data-download). Exact filters, releases, attribution, provenance and the distinction between NPS administrative boundaries and mostly PAD-US park land holdings are documented in [PARK_BOUNDARIES.md](PARK_BOUNDARIES.md). Data was verified on 6 September 2026; these are prepared snapshots, not live or complete state-park coverage.

## FCC mobile coverage: North Carolina pilot

The initial prepared layers are T-Mobile (`130403`) **LTE 5/1 Mbps**, **5G 7/1 Mbps** and **5G 35/3 Mbps**, outdoor stationary, reporting period **2025-12-31**, acquired 6 September 2026 from the [FCC National Broadband Map](https://broadbandmap.fcc.gov/data-download). Their original catalog file IDs are `1773403`, `1773720` and `1773914`. Exact `providerid`, `technology`, `mindown`, `minup`, and `environmnt` filters are recorded in the offline import configs. Outdoor stationary includes environment values **0 and 1** under the [FCC field definitions](https://help.bdc.fcc.gov/hc/en-us/articles/6047425308187-Formatting-Mobile-Broadband-Availability-Coverage-Maps).

North Carolina is the only completed geography in these published layers. Other states, territories, carrier combinations and countries are unknown/unpublished. Acquired but unconverted files are not selectable. The independent state footprint is [Census TIGER 2025 STATE](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2025/STATE/), exact `STATEFP=37`, explicitly transformed from NAD83. FCC/Census boundary rounding excess was reviewed: LTE 120.343 m², 5G 35/3 45.405 m², each below both configured caps of 300 m² and 1e-8 of coverage area. Only these bounded rounding differences were clipped; reports retain the measurements and source hashes.

Coverage means carrier-reported modeled availability under the selected network, speed and environment. It is not measured signal strength, live reception, actual speed or subscriber access. Download dates and inner archive modification dates do not replace the reporting date. Native 256px XYZ tiles end at zoom 9 (~250m ground pixels at 35° N), preserve polygon holes within raster resolution, and magnify at closer zooms. Transparent out-of-scope areas do not establish an absence of service. No authoritative point query is offered.

Source checksums, processing versions, acquisition/processing times, native zoom range, declared geography and limits are included in the immutable manifest. Raw archives and GeoPackages stay in the ignored offline research directory. Map reload fetches only the currently published prepared release; it never sends the FCC credential or performs GIS conversion. See README for preparation, validation and rollback.

## Map layers

- [Esri World Imagery](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer) provides the default mosaic. Credit Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. Capture dates vary geographically; it is not live imagery.
- [OpenStreetMap raster tiles](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) provide streets with © OpenStreetMap contributors attribution. Standard browser caching and referrer behavior are retained. No offline prefetch or bulk download is implemented.
- [Esri World Topographic Map](https://server.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Topo_Map/MapServer) provides surface cartography; credit its contributors. This imagery is draped over the separate terrain mesh.
- [Esri boundaries and places](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/Reference/World_Boundaries_and_Places/MapServer) provides the optional labels overlay.
- Cesium's bundled public-domain Natural Earth II tiles remain beneath every network map. They are intentionally enlarged past native zoom 2 whenever a selected provider is loading, unavailable, or has no closer tile, so the globe keeps a coarse geographic view instead of going blank. Esri's canonical missing-tile images are discarded so Cesium can retain the closest real ancestor tile.

## Interpretation

Satellite positions are estimates from orbital elements, not live telemetry. Element epoch and retrieval time remain separate. Elements older than three days get a warning; those more than fourteen days from epoch are hidden from the globe. Maneuvers, drag, and uncertain elements can make the real position differ. No numerical precision guarantee is made.

Aircraft and ships use constant-speed, constant-course great-circle estimates for at most 60 and 120 seconds respectively, then freeze the estimate. Missing speed or course retains the reported position. Aircraft altitude is held constant between reports. Missing altitude is shown as unavailable and uses ground height only as a rendering fallback. Aircraft geometric or barometric altitude is approximate in the globe's height system. Old reports are visibly dimmed and expose their timestamp on selection. Traffic remains in real time when the orbital simulation slider moves.

Assessment owners, registered legal owners, taxpayers, and ultimate beneficial owners are distinct. Missing or suppressed names remain unavailable. Parcel geometry and address overlays load after the camera settles; ownership inquiries require Get data. Detailed limits and source roles are described below.

Global maritime AIS is not included: research identified account-based providers such as [AISStream](https://aisstream.io/documentation), which require server-side credentials and a different streaming integration. Aircraft reception coverage is also incomplete and depends on receivers. No unavailable source is replaced by synthetic “live” traffic.

## Terrain, address tiles, and land inquiry (September 2026 update)

- Real elevation: [Esri World Elevation 3D Terrain3D](https://elevation3d.arcgis.com/arcgis/rest/services/WorldElevation3D/Terrain3D/ImageServer), loaded with Cesium ArcGISTiledElevationTerrainProvider. No Cesium ion token is used. Imagery changes preserve the same terrain provider.
- [Overture addresses](https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/addresses/) use [official release 2026-08-19.0 PMTiles](https://overturemaps-extras-us-west-2.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/tiles/2026-08-19.0/addresses.pmtiles), via bounded HTTP Range requests, never the full archive. Read the z14 address layer, preserve units, address levels and contributing dataset/license/record IDs. Up to 16 intersecting tiles per view, three requests at a time, a 45-second budget and 4,000 displayed points. Successful snapshots cache for one day; partial tile failures for only 20 seconds. Coverage is extensive but incomplete and cannot certify mailable addresses.
- Parcel overlays query official service ID lists then geometry in batches that respect each source's record cap. Explicit terrain-clamped ring polylines make boundaries visible over all base maps; holes and multipart parcels are preserved. Boundary field lists exclude owner names. The independent toggle stays enabled across zoom and map changes. Queries use a 45-second budget per source, up to three concurrent sources, 2,000 shapes per source, and a one-hour cache.
- Property inquiries begin only after Get data. Available point-intersection APIs return up to 100 records and explicitly flag provider truncation. Unit matching is not inferred from a shared coordinate. Source privacy flags and withheld/placeholder names are respected; Dallas taxpayer fields and the older Florida assessment owner role are labeled separately. API evidence caches for five minutes and retains its acquisition time.
- Linked-page extraction is bounded to two pages and 512 KB per page, honors robots rules, accepts explicitly allowed hosts, and does not bypass logins, CAPTCHA, fees or access restrictions. It matches labelled parcel identifiers or the complete selected address within one record in a table, definition list, or JSON-LD property object. Unrelated page text, listing agents and sellers are not treated as ownership. Conflicting identities/names require manual review. The whole inquiry has a 60-second budget and streams partial checks/results.
- Country-specific registry links are copied from the supplied research catalog in lib/data/property-directory.json. Countries without an automated adapter offer these links; they do not pretend to have completed a web-wide search. Neither addresses nor ownership have universal free global coverage.
- Server response reads and in-memory caches are bounded for the hosted Worker. Source outages, empty responses and missing jurisdiction coverage are distinct states. No Nominatim or public main Overpass automated lookup is used.

## OpenCellID estimated cell locations

The separate `/cells` release contains 694,839 validated records from MCC exports 310–314, downloaded on 2026-09-06 through the existing offline Transmitter Builder adapter. Coordinates are estimates; records are neither a physical-tower census nor coverage evidence. Physical country is not inferred from MCC. All five requested files succeeded; 68 network allocation keys and GSM/LTE/NR/UMTS are represented. Current allocation labels are attributed to the official [U.S. IMSI administrator](https://imsiadmin.com/assignments/hni/) (snapshot 2026-09-06); 11 unmapped records retain raw-code fallbacks. Deprecated source fields do not drive rendering.

Data © [OpenCellID contributors](https://opencellid.org/), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Attribution and source terms are retained in the globe, controls, inspector and prepared metadata. Browser requests use bounded local pages and never contain the OpenCellID token. See [CELL_LOCATIONS.md](CELL_LOCATIONS.md) for per-MCC counts, source releases, measured costs, limitations, and the offline refresh procedure. The pre-existing `/radio` and FCC datasets were preserved.

## Unified place and object search

The pre-existing global text index does not include the new cell records; use their map/network/technology controls.

The local unified search uses prepared navigation metadata for all current radio and park records, current loaded satellite/traffic/address/parcel snapshots, the parcel-source registry and ready FCC coverage selections. Online place/address lookup uses Photon with OpenStreetMap attribution (ODbL). It is explicitly submitted, capped at 12 results, cached for 24 hours and subject to the public demo's reasonable-use limits and availability. No current precise map center is sent to Photon. The app does not claim every address, worldwide AIS, every satellite, or nationwide text-searchable parcel IDs. See [UNIFIED_SEARCH.md](UNIFIED_SEARCH.md) for exact scope, endpoint, sizes, refresh and verification.
